# Brand imagery

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, September 2026. These are illustrative scenes, not photographs of EightVo staff or customers. Original brand JPGs are preserved for rollback.

## Assets and prompt direction

- `public/images/ai-products.webp`: EightVo hero; people building connected, simple AI applications on a laptop and phone. Premium editorial illustration, tactile matte materials, teal/sage/ivory/gold, portrait framing with caption space below; no logos, readable interface data, or robot imagery.
- `public/images/expert-collaboration.webp`: domain experts and engineers working together around a laptop, tablet and movable workflow cards. Warm natural light, matching palette, landscape composition, no claimed real staff identities.
- `public/images/canadian-journey.webp`: a couple planning with a tablet and checklist, a symbolic pathway toward Toronto, and a maple leaf motif. Matching palette, landscape composition; no government seals, visas, or promises of approval.

Hero uses the AI-products image. Approach, About, Careers, Contact, Blog and project enquiries use collaboration imagery. MyCanJourney uses its own journey scene. Page banners preserve complete scenes rather than cropping people into a shallow strip. AI-created scene captions identify the nature of the imagery.

Generated originals were visually inspected, then resized and encoded as WebP at quality 82. No external image hosts are required. Local clean install and production build passed; existing CSS token syntax warnings are unrelated to this change. Browser visual QA remains pending.
